Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Subscription Hunter ("the App") is a personal subscription tracking application. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All subscription information you enter is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. No data is sent to any server or third party.

Internet Usage

The App connects to the internet solely to load company logos (brand icons) from Google's public favicon service. No personal data is included in these requests.

Notifications

The App may request permission to send local notifications to remind you of upcoming subscription renewals. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not involve any external servers.

Data Export

You may export your subscription data at any time. The exported file is shared directly from your device and is under your full control.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

Any future changes to this policy will be reflected on this page.

Contact

If you have questions, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
